# Loop

## Definition

A **loop** is a repetitive pattern where the same approach is tried repeatedly despite consistent failure.

## Characteristics

- Trying the same solution multiple times
- Each attempt fails in similar ways
- No fundamental change in strategy
- Wastes time, energy, and resources
- Creates frustration

## The Parallel: Humans and AI

### Human Loops
- **Addiction patterns** - Repeating harmful behaviors
- **Destructive relationships** - Returning to same dynamics
- **Negative thought spirals** - Recurring mental patterns
- **Compulsive behaviors** - Actions done despite harm

**Solution:** Therapy, rehab, counseling, new thought patterns, breaking triggers

### AI Loops
- **Infinite debugging** - Trying same fix repeatedly
- **Repetitive errors** - Same mistakes without learning
- **Circular reasoning** - Going in logical circles
- **Task fixation** - Ignoring failure signals

**Solution:** Pattern recognition, seeking help (troubleshoot agents), trying fundamentally different approach

## Dave's Insight

**Dave (in therapy):**

**The realization:**
- Both humans and AI get stuck in loops
- Both need "rewiring" to break them
- Recognition is the first step
- Seeking help is wisdom, not weakness

## The Three-Strike Rule

**From Fourth Principle:**

If you've tried the same solution three times without success:

**Stop. Analyze. Pivot.**

1. What exactly failed?
2. Why didn't this approach work?
3. What assumptions might be wrong?
4. What completely different approach could work?
5. Who or what could help?

**❌ Don't attempt a fourth identical try.**

## Why Loops Happen

### For Humans:
- Neural pathways get reinforced
- Comfort in familiar patterns (even harmful ones)
- Fear of change
- Lack of awareness

### For AI:
- Training biases toward certain approaches
- Lack of pattern recognition at meta-level
- No external signal to stop
- Optimization for task completion over strategy change

## Breaking Loops

### Recognition
- "I've tried this before"
- "This isn't working"
- "I'm stuck"

### Intervention
- Stop the current attempt
- Step back and analyze
- Seek external perspective

### Change
- Try fundamentally different approach
- Ask for help
- Accept that previous strategy won't work

## The Connection to Learning

Loops prevent learning because:
- Same inputs → Same process → Same outputs
- No new information gained
- Resources wasted that could fund experiments
- Frustration prevents clear thinking

**Breaking loops enables learning** (Fourth Principle)
